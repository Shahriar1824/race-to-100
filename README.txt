## 🚀 How to Play

### Basic Rules:
1. **Goal**: Be the first player to reach **exactly 100 points**
2. **On Your Turn**: Roll two dice and choose how to combine them:
   - Create a two-digit number (e.g., 3 & 5 → 35 or 53)
   - Add them together (3 + 5 = 8)
   - Multiply them (3 × 5 = 15)
3. **Bust Rule**: If you go over 100, you subtract the overage from your score!

### Winning Strategy:
- Plan your moves carefully as you approach 100
- Consider all four operation options each turn
- Avoid going over 100 to prevent busting!

## 🎯 Game Modes

### 1. **Two Players**
- Play with a friend on the same device
- Take turns rolling dice and choosing operations

### 2. **Play vs Computer**
- Challenge the AI with three difficulty levels:
  - **Easy**: Makes random valid moves
  - **Medium**: Smart moves with occasional randomness
  - **Hard**: Always chooses optimal moves
