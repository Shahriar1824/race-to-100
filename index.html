Race to 100

Roll, calculate, and be the first to reach exactly 100 points!

How to Play

1
Start your turn by clicking the "Roll Dice" button.
2
Combine your dice by choosing one of the four operations after rolling.
3
Reach exactly 100 to win! If you go over 100, you'll bust and subtract points.
4
Operations available: Create a two-digit number, add, or multiply the dice values.
